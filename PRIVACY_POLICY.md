# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Secure Domain Search browser extension ("Extension"), provided by CodeViews ("we", "us", or "our").

## 1. Overview

Secure Domain Search is designed to be privacy-first. We do not personally operate a backend for this Extension and we do not collect or store your search history on our own servers.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information from you.

The Extension may store data locally in your browser extension storage, including:
- your preferences and settings
- your wishlist entries

This local data stays on your device/browser profile.

## 3. How Domain Checks Work

To check domain availability, the Extension sends requests directly to third-party infrastructure, including:
- public DNS-over-HTTPS providers (for DNS checks)
- registry RDAP services (for registration status checks)

Because these requests are made to third-party services, those providers may process technical request data (such as IP address and request metadata) under their own privacy policies.

## 4. No Central Storage by Us

We do not maintain central servers for this Extension's search functionality and do not centrally store your domain query data.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Since Extension data is stored locally, you can remove it by deleting/uninstalling the add-on from your browser (or clearing extension storage through browser controls).

## 6. Children's Privacy

No minimum age is required to use this Extension. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly through the Extension.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable steps in the Extension's design to minimize data collection. However, no method of transmission or storage is fully secure.

## 8. International Use

The Extension may be used globally. Third-party DNS/RDAP providers may operate in different jurisdictions.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.
